[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3265298940]Progression: Hospitality[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Overhauls and integrates hospitality mods into a more complete package, on its own Hospitality research tab.
[*]Adds a merchant box, a low-tech register for running a shop or restaurant before electricity.
[*]Gives clearer names to some buildings, such as the vending shelf, the fossil displays and the market tents.
[*]Hides duplicate museum barriers and display cases when several mods overlap.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Hospitality adds up to [b]3 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork cuts that to [b]at most 2[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Basic retail[/b] (Neolithic)
[*][b]Museums[/b] (Medieval), which also holds paleontology when Biomes! Fossils is active
[/list]
Everything else unlocks with the research it already builds on:
[list]
[*]Slot machines: [b]Microelectronics[/b]
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]Hospitality: Vending machines, Casino, Spa and Storefront
[*]Cash Register and Gastronomy
[*]Museums and Biomes! Fossils
[*]Hospital
[*]Medieval Overhaul
[*]ReBuild: Doors and Corners
[*]Roo's Painting Expansion
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged or removed, so swapping Progression: Hospitality for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
